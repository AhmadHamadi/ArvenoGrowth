How it works

  1. Make a permission-based introduction. Ask the business if they are open to being introduced, then email both parties or have them mention your name when they request an audit.
  2. We confirm the referral. We’ll acknowledge the introduction and confirm whether it is eligible before a proposal is accepted.
  3. We pay from collected service fees. Once the referred business becomes a client and pays, we calculate your share from eligible recurring service fees.

What counts toward the referral share

The referral share is based on recurring Arveno Growth service fees received from the referred client, after discounts and credits. Advertising spend paid to Google or another platform, one-time setup or project fees, taxes, refunded amounts, and unpaid invoices are excluded. The share is paid monthly while the eligible client account remains active and current, subject to the written referral confirmation.

The standard rate is 15%. A higher rate, up to 20%, must be agreed in writing before the introduction is made. Referrals are for new prospective clients that were not already in active discussions with Arveno Growth. We may decline an introduction if the business is already a client or prospect, or if the introduction was made without permission.

This program is a referral thank-you, not an employment or agency relationship. Referrers are responsible for any tax obligations that apply to payments they receive. We do not share a referred business’s confidential campaign or financial information with the referrer.
