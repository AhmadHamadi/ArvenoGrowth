Working together

Budget, ads, and measurement

How much should a contractor spend on marketing?

There is no reliable universal amount. Consider gross profit per job, close rate, service capacity, target work, and the cost of reaching a qualified customer. Our budget planning guide explains the inputs to work through.

Can you promise a particular number of leads?

No. Lead volume and quality depend on factors such as local demand, competition, budget, season, offer, response time, and capacity. We set up measurement and use actual enquiries and sales outcomes to guide decisions.

What does “we work for free” mean?

For eligible campaigns, if agreed, trackable gross profit has not covered the agreed advertising spend and our fee by day 90, we waive our fee and keep providing the agreed services at no charge until the written break-even target is reached. The signed agreement sets the budget, tracking, calculation, scope, and any time limit. Platform ad spend is separate and is not refundable.

Can you guarantee the top of Google or AI search results?

No. Search engines decide which results to show. We focus on technical access, clear business information, useful content, and accurate measurement rather than guaranteed placements.

Local search and trust

Will you publish a page for every city in America?

No. We create pages where the business has meaningful coverage and can provide useful local detail. Swapping city names across thin pages does not make them helpful and can resemble doorway content.

Can remote marketing help with “near me” searches?

We can improve the website, profile, and campaign for a real business and its true service area. Local map results also depend on the searcher’s distance and business prominence; remote agency support does not create a nearby office.

Can I see your client work?

The homepage shows work for Seven Stones Landscape, IKAD Mechanical, and Tridan Contracting. Seven Stones revenue figures are identified as supplied figures, with the verification limits stated. We distinguish website and campaign work from measured financial results; a completed website alone does not prove a revenue gain. Review the client work.

Website content and AI search

How do you measure AI search visibility?

We start with a defined set of relevant questions, a named search service, and a record of the date and market. We distinguish a brand mention from a linked citation and a referral that becomes an enquiry. Results can vary between responses, so an isolated mention is not a stable ranking. The AI visibility service explains the work and reporting limits.

Will more city pages help every local search?

A location page is useful when it contains specific information a customer needs about a genuine service territory. Repeating the same text with a different city name does not answer that need. Local search also depends on relevance and distance. We plan coverage around real operating areas, supported by the local SEO work, rather than implying offices in places where the business has none.

What photos should a service business use?

Choose photographs that help customers understand the work. Approved project photos can demonstrate the team's own delivery; licensed stock photography can explain a subject but should not be presented as a completed client project. Use a descriptive filename, useful alt text, and an efficient image size. Our website service includes planning the supporting content.

Choosing a growth partner

How do I compare two marketing agencies fairly?

Compare scope, service fees, advertising spend, ownership, reporting, and cancellation terms separately. Ask each agency to explain how its proposed work addresses your specific bottleneck. A larger list of deliverables is not necessarily a better fit. Use the agency comparison guide to prepare for the conversation.

What should a trustworthy agency show before I sign?

Look for a clear written scope, accurate examples, transparent costs, and a measurement plan. Ask what a case-study figure represents and which period it covers. A trustworthy explanation separates enquiries, booked work, revenue, and profit instead of treating them as interchangeable.

What is the difference between a marketing agency and a growth partner?

Marketing work focuses on attracting and converting demand. A growth partnership can also examine service priorities, capacity, sales follow-up, measurement, and practical AI workflows. The useful distinction is the agreed work and accountability, not the label. Our growth consulting page explains that broader scope.

Can I keep my existing website and improve it?

Often, yes. Start by checking whether customers can understand the services, use the contact path, and find the right information on a phone. A rebuild is appropriate when the current platform or structure prevents necessary improvements; it should not be the automatic answer to every marketing problem.

How do I know which plan fits my business?

Start with the constraint. If you need to test paid demand, discuss lead generation. If your public service information and search visibility need work, discuss the visibility plan. If both are priorities and the business has capacity, review the combined growth scope. The plans page makes those paths easier to compare.

Qualified leads and campaign decisions

What is a qualified lead for a service business?

It is an enquiry that meets your agreed criteria, such as the right service, territory, customer type, and timing. Define those criteria before reporting. A phone call or form submission is a contact event; qualification requires additional information from the business.

Why can a cheap lead still be expensive for my business?

A low-cost enquiry can consume staff time without producing suitable work. Compare qualification, booking, and job outcomes alongside cost per lead. Acquisition cost and the margin of completed work are more useful than choosing a campaign because its raw lead price is the lowest.

Can a $700–$1,100 monthly ad budget be useful?

It may support a focused test, depending on the service, territory, competition, and actual lead costs. Keep the scope narrow enough to learn something useful. Separate management fees from media spend and compare results with your own records. The calculator uses fixed assumptions rather than a promise of volume.

Should I advertise every service I offer?

Not necessarily. Start with services that fit your capacity, economics, and customer demand. Separate offers when their search intent or booking journey differs. Adding many services to a modest budget can make it harder to understand which work is producing suitable enquiries.

Why do Google Ads conversions differ from booked jobs?

A conversion may record a call or form event, while a booked job happens later after qualification and scheduling. Duplicates, existing customers, spam, and undecided enquiries can affect the comparison. Connect platform events with your business records before describing them as sales.

When should I increase my advertising budget?

Increase spending when the evidence supports it and the business can handle additional work. Check response times, lead fit, close rates, and capacity first. If good enquiries are being lost during follow-up, fixing that process may be more useful than buying additional traffic.

AI search and useful website content

What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

AEO usually means answer engine optimization: organizing accurate, useful information so answer-based search experiences can interpret it. SEO covers broader search discovery and technical foundations. For Google’s AI features, the underlying SEO requirements still apply; there is no separate guaranteed-placement technique.

Will adding many FAQs automatically improve rankings?

No. Add questions because customers need the answers. Make each answer specific, accurate, and connected to the relevant service or guide. Repeated generic questions across dozens of pages create volume without adding much value. Read our AI search guide for a practical approach.

Should every page target the same marketing keywords?

No. Give each page a distinct purpose: a service offer, an industry application, a planning guide, or an answer to a specific question. Link related pages where it helps the reader. The keyword research guide explains how to map intent without duplicating pages.

Do longer articles always perform better?

No. A page should contain enough useful information to answer its question well. Expand missing explanations, examples, and decision criteria instead of padding the word count. Keep headings specific so visitors can find the part they need.

Can stock photos help image SEO?

Relevant licensed photos can support a page, with descriptive filenames, useful alt text, and appropriate image sizing. They do not prove original work or local experience. Our website photography guide explains how to use them clearly.

How will we know whether organic enquiries improve?

Connect search-page performance with enquiry and job records where possible. Review relevant clicks, landing pages, lead fit, and confirmed outcomes over comparable periods. An increase in impressions alone does not establish that the business is receiving more suitable work.

Websites, follow-up, and AI systems

What should happen after someone submits my website form?

Show an accurate confirmation, notify the right person, and create a record with an owner and next action. Test the entire path, including delivery to the inbox or CRM. A form that displays success but loses the notification is not a working enquiry process.

Can AI help us respond to leads faster?

It can help acknowledge requests, summarize information, route enquiries, or remind staff to follow up. Give it a defined role and a human handoff. Do not let an acknowledgement imply a confirmed booking, price, or technical assessment that nobody has approved.

Do we need a new CRM before improving follow-up?

Not always. Begin with the process: ownership, statuses, next steps, and reliable records. A CRM can support those habits as volume and handoffs grow, but installing software alone does not create them. See the missed-call follow-up guide.

What should an auto repair marketing report include?

Separate new enquiries, appointments booked, attended visits, and completed repair orders. Identify existing customers and review the service mix. A busy phone can still leave bays underfilled if the enquiries cannot be scheduled or do not fit the shop. See auto repair marketing.

What should an HVAC marketing report include?

Distinguish repair, maintenance, and installation opportunities, then connect them with qualified calls, booked visits, and confirmed work. Seasonal demand and technician capacity affect interpretation. Read the HVAC marketing page for the service-specific approach.

Can you help plan GoHighLevel CRM workflows?

We can review how enquiries enter the pipeline, who owns them, which stages represent real business events, and how follow-up stops or hands over to a person. The agreed implementation depends on your account and existing systems. Our GoHighLevel guide explains the planning and validation process.
