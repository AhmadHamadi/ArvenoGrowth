# Arveno Growth website messaging guide

Every public page follows this guide. Owner direction (Nov 14, 2026): sell the outcome (more booked appointments, more jobs, more revenue), not the mechanics. Arveno is a growth partner and an AI company. All of our systems together build a stronger online presence, and that is what brings in more jobs.

## The one idea

**More booked jobs from the people already looking for you.**

All of the work feeds one result:

| They want | We do (the proof, never the headline) |
|---|---|
| More people find them | Website, Google Ads, local SEO and Google Business Profile, AI search visibility |
| More of those people book | Fast replies to every call and enquiry (AI follow-up), reminders, quote follow-up |
| More revenue they can see | Tracking from first call to paid job, plain reports |

Write the outcome first. Put the mechanism in the next sentence, as the reason to believe it.

> Outcome → because → mechanism.
> "Fewer jobs go to the next contractor on Google, because every new enquiry gets a reply within minutes, even when you're on a job."

## Positioning line (use as written)

- Short: **Growth partner and AI company for contractors and local service businesses.**
- Long: **We're a growth partner and AI company for contractors and local service businesses. We connect your website, Google, ads and follow-up into one system, so more people find you, more of them book, and you can see which jobs your marketing paid for.**
- Footer: **Growth partner and AI company for contractors and local service businesses across the US and Canada.**

Don't use: "AI consultants" as the main label, "agency" for ourselves, "solutions", "leverage", "seamless", "unlock", "supercharge", "transform", "cutting-edge", "AI-powered" in a heading.

## Words

- Use: booked jobs, booked appointments, estimates on the calendar, calls, revenue, the work you want, your area, your crew.
- Say AI where it makes a claim believable ("a reply goes out in minutes, even at 9 PM") or where the page is about AI. Don't stack it in headings.
- "Qualify", "qualification", "CRM", "pipeline", "speed to lead": body copy on the relevant service pages only, never in a hero or heading.
- Grade 5–7 reading level. Short sentences. "You" and "your business".

## Calls to action

- Primary everywhere: **Get my free growth review**
- Secondary: **Call (289) 489-1167**
- Under the main button: "Free. A person reviews your setup, then you pick a time to talk. No pressure, no obligation."
- The review promises: **where more booked jobs can come from**. Section CTAs: "See where your next jobs can come from."

## Honesty rules (unchanged)

- Promise the work and the direction, never a number. "More booked jobs" is the aim we work toward. No "guaranteed leads", "double your revenue", "money back", "risk-free", "pays for itself".
- Client figures stay labelled client-reported. No invented reviews, counts or stats.
- The guarantee is a written fee pause (see `GUARANTEE-COPY.md`), with its conditions next to it.
- Rankings and AI answers: we improve the odds; platforms decide.
