# Fresh keyword and content review

**Reviewed:** 8 October 2026

**Scope:** Current `feature/arveno-growth-website` branch; content architecture for a US/Canada marketing, AI, and growth agency serving contractors, local service businesses, and auto repair shops.

**Method:** Read-only review of the site source, current keyword map, SEO audit and coverage/case-study notes, local SEO/AI guidance in Desktop Skills and Desktop SEO Agent, plus a small sample of current competitor pages and official search guidance. No Search Console, Analytics, keyword-volume, rank-tracking, or client campaign exports were supplied. Candidate queries below are qualitative hypotheses, not volume or difficulty estimates.

## Findings to act on first

1. **The site already has a meaningful content base.** There are service pages, 12 trade pages, 15 articles, FAQ, plans, coverage, and a separate case-study page. The next move should be intent cleanup and a few high-value gaps, not mass-producing more blogs.
2. **Most service/blog pairs are sensible if their intent stays distinct.** The clearest overlap risks are the broad contractor lead-generation guide versus the service/industry hubs; the agency-selection article versus the FAQ; and local SEO, keyword research, and service-area guidance. These are likely overlap risks, not proven ranking cannibalization; GSC query-to-page data is needed to confirm.
3. **Auto repair local-search coverage has been strengthened.** A dedicated Google Business Profile and local SEO checklist now supports the commercial auto-repair solution page and the existing Google Ads guide. The next useful gaps are shop-specific website/booking and attribution content, but only if customer demand or Search Console data supports them.
4. **AI visibility content is strongest when it describes normal search-readiness work.** Keep the service page and explainer distinct as commercial versus informational. Avoid claims of guaranteed citations, special AI schema, required chunk lengths, or citation-rate uplift.
5. **Protect evidence quality in growth/benchmark content.** The case-study records say several revenue figures are owner/client supplied and still lack verified dates, source records, or—in one case—currency. Do not turn those into independent benchmarks, typical results, or ROI claims.
6. **Verify testimonial wording before publication.** Current homepage/testimonials source attributes the Seven Stones quote to “Arveno Growth.” The prior site/review text referenced “Trade Leads Marketing.” Confirm the client-approved original wording; do not silently rewrite a business name inside quotation marks during a rebrand. If the wording must change, obtain approval or paraphrase outside the quotation.

## Desktop SEO and AI materials review

The installed `agency-seo` guidance in `Desktop/Skills/skills/SEO/agency-seo/` and `/Users/ahmadhamadi/.codex/skills/agency-seo/` is the best operating baseline in the materials reviewed: use the client’s actual market/data, label evidence versus hypotheses, do not invent volume or outcome claims, prioritize useful customer content, and treat AI visibility as a measured outcome rather than a score or guarantee. Its Search and AI references were dated 6 October 2026.

The Desktop `SEO Agent/seo-agent/` pack has useful process controls: its shared protocol calls for evidence and confidence labels; Agent 01 forbids invented volumes; Agent 02 calls for intent-to-page mapping and duplicate checks; the content brief says to map content to a real buyer decision; Agent 22 emphasizes primary sources for regulations and statistics. Keep those safeguards.

However, several pack rules overstate what is established by current search-provider guidance and should not be enforced as ranking requirements:

- Agents 02/04/11/21 prescribe fixed 40–75-word “atomic capsules,” no links inside capsules, fixed article lengths, mandatory visible update dates, three unique data points per page, citation-rate multipliers, and mandatory FAQ/schema patterns. These are not Google requirements and can produce mechanical filler if applied indiscriminately.
- Agent 02 and Agent 21 contain dramatic traffic/AI-citation percentages, source-overlap rates, and cross-engine rankings without adequate primary-source support in the pack. Do not repeat those numbers or forecast them for Arveno.
- Agent 21 describes `llms.txt` as beneficial hygiene; current Google guidance says no AI machine-readable files or special schema are needed for its AI features and that Google Search ignores `llms.txt`. Other providers must be checked against their own documentation.
- Agent 22’s primary-source gate is valuable for legal, regulatory, statistical, and credential claims. Its universal requirements for page-visible reference blocks, `<cite>` tags, ClaimReview schema, and dates should be applied by claim type, not as blanket search ranking rules.

Current official guidance checked for this review: [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says normal search eligibility and helpful, crawlable content apply and there is no special schema; [Google’s AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says no fixed chunking/length or need to target every query variation; [Google’s generative-AI content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) warns that scaled pages without added value can violate spam policy. [OpenAI’s publisher FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq) distinguishes `OAI-SearchBot` search discovery from `GPTBot` training preferences.

## Evidence, geography, and verification limits

- `docs/SEO-AUDIT.md` states that Search Console, Analytics, Bing Webmaster, and server-log data were not supplied. I found no new exports in this repo. Therefore actual query demand, organic cannibalization, rankings, AI citations, and lead contribution remain unverified.
- `src/brand-config.json`, homepage, and coverage content position remote service across the US and Canada, with an Ontario-specific page. This supports national/remote coverage messaging only. It does not support a local-office claim, city-page expansion, or local Maps ranking claim.
- Official [Keyword Planner](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243) can supply market/date-specific estimates; record country/region, network, date range, and currency before prioritizing by volume. Search Console can identify queries and the pages shown for them; use its query and page views to test the overlap hypotheses above ([query/page reporting](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17010961)).
- Google’s [Business Profile representation policies](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) require accurate business details and precise actual service areas. Google’s [Local Services Ads eligibility](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6230381) says screening varies by service category and country.
- The Seven Stones screenshots in the current repository are a valuable proof asset and are already presented in `/case-studies/` with explicit limits. Confirm that the screenshots and client name are approved for continued public use. The separate client-reported revenue calculation still needs currency, dates, and source records before it can support a quantified marketing result.

## Suggested measurement after the production domain is settled

1. Verify the correct domain in Search Console and export query/page/country data after enough time for a baseline. Compare branded versus non-branded queries and filter for contractor, auto repair, agency, SEO, Ads, AI, and workflow terms.
2. For each suspected overlap, use Search Console’s query filter, then inspect the pages shown. Retarget only if two pages serve the same intent and the data supports consolidation.
3. Use Keyword Planner for a small list of commercial variants in named US and Canadian target markets. Save the raw export and settings; do not rely on unlocalized web estimates.
4. Track lead actions by page/service and, where the client CRM allows, qualified enquiries, estimates/appointments, won work, cancellations, and revenue. Separate client-reported data from verified records.
5. For AI discovery, freeze a short set of real buyer prompts and record engine, surface, country/language, date, repetitions, exact citations, and referral/conversion evidence. Do not call a robots change or a schema change proof of AI visibility.
