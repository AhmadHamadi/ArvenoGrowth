# Current guarantee wording and claim controls

**Reviewed:** 19 November 2026. This replaces the October 8, 2026 summary below, which incorrectly described the current setup term as 14 days. The current public homepage says setup in 7 days or setup is free. Check the signed agreement version and live copy before quoting terms.

## Current written terms for new agreements

### Setup timing

For current agreements (version 4+), Arveno agrees to complete setup within 7 days of the agreed Setup Start Date. If setup is not complete by then, the setup fee is waived and any unpaid setup installments are canceled. Older signed agreements retain the term in those agreements (14 days). Do not promise a refund of a setup payment already collected unless that remedy is expressly confirmed in the specific signed agreement.

**Copy discrepancy to resolve:** the live homepage currently says the setup fee may be “waived or refunded.” The current internal offer context and this campaign’s pitch use the waiver remedy. Confirm the remedy in signed agreement v4+ and align the public page before making further edits to the guarantee.

### Conditional monthly service-fee pause

The client pays the first three monthly service fees after setup. If verified CRM-tracked gross profit from eligible projects does not cover those service fees by the end of the first three paid months, monthly service billing pauses while Arveno continues the included work without a monthly service fee until the target is reached, subject to the agreement. Regular billing resumes only after the target is reached, unless the agreement has ended.

The target excludes setup fees, ad spend, taxes, software and third-party costs. Fees already paid are not refunded. The safeguard applies while the client keeps the agreed ad budget and CRM tracking active, shares accurate job revenue and cost records, provides required access, and replies to new enquiries within one business day. The signed agreement controls the eligible projects, attribution, measurement and exact conditions.

## Proposed ad-budget threshold is not yet an approved guarantee term

The owner mentioned a possible $800–$1,000 advertising budget alongside pre-launch research and adding terms to the contract. Currency, period (monthly or total), required duration, scope of research, and consequence if results fall short are not defined here. Do not insert this number as a guarantee, minimum, or eligibility rule until the owner confirms those details and the signed agreement is updated. Advertising spend remains separate from Arveno service fees.

## Public wording guardrails

- Do not call the monthly safeguard “money back,” “risk-free,” a refund, or a guaranteed profit. It is a conditional pause of future monthly service billing; previously paid fees are not refunded.
- Describe setup simply as “Setup in 7 days, or setup is free” only where the current agreement version applies. Do not use the retired 14-day headline for new agreements.
- Do not promise rankings, AI citations, lead counts or revenue. The safeguard is not a promise that every business will earn a target profit.
- Keep the full terms and eligibility conditions beside the offer and in the signed agreement.
