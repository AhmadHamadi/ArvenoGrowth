# Guarantee wording and research

Reviewed October 6, 2026. Headline: **Make your money back. Or we work for free.**

The offer remains a service-fee waiver after a 90-day measurement period, not an upfront free service or cash refund. The nearby eligibility line retains the period, agreed advertising budget and tracking, gross-profit calculation, separate advertising spend, and written terms. An orange shield badge emphasizes accountability rather than the length of the period. The audit CTA remains the next step.

## Observed agency approaches

- [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/) leads with revenue growth and connects marketing activity to financial outcomes.
- [KlientBoost](https://www.klientboost.com/) emphasizes goals and accountability, with a free marketing plan as the conversion action.
- [Disruptive Advertising](https://disruptiveadvertising.com/) presents a conditional 90-day results guarantee with a free audit CTA. Its advertised refund remedy differs from Arveno's continued-service remedy; the latter is stated explicitly.

These are examples of positioning, not evidence that one headline is the highest-converting option.

[Nielsen Norman Group's concise/scannable/objective writing study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/) supports readable, direct copy. It is a usability study, not a guarantee-headline conversion trial. The recommendation is an editorial inference: lead with the desired financial outcome, specify the agency's responsibility, and keep eligibility easy to find.

Avoid claiming zero risk, unconditional profitability, or no payment from day one: those claims would not describe this offer. The calculator is a separate estimate and does not define contractual performance.
