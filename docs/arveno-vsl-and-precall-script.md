# Arveno Growth VSL and booked-call assets

**Prepared:** October 23, 2026  
**Audience:** Owners of U.S. and Canadian contractor and local service businesses  
**Goal:** Help the right prospect understand Arveno's approach, book a free 30-minute consultation, and arrive knowing what the call is for.

## Funnel recommendation

Use two short, distinct videos. The VSL explains the offer before booking; the pre-call briefing helps a person who already booked prepare and attend. Keep booking open and visible. Do not make the VSL a required gate.

| Asset | Placement | Target length | Main job |
|---|---|---:|---|
| VSL | Homepage or focused growth page, beside the booking CTA | 1:45–2:15 | Explain the problem Arveno solves, the connected system, package paths, and what the consultation will do |
| Pre-call briefing | Booking confirmation page, immediately after scheduling | 0:45–1:00 | Confirm the appointment details, request a real attendance confirmation, and explain what to expect |
| Reminder clip (optional) | Email or consented SMS link on the day before | 0:15–0:25 | Re-state time, meeting link, and one easy reschedule action |
| Short-form cutdowns | Instagram Reels and TikTok; link to the consultation page | 0:20–0:35 initial test range | One specific buyer problem, one practical insight, one CTA |

These are starting durations, not universal conversion benchmarks. Wistia's 2026 analysis of 13M+ uploaded videos found engagement generally higher for shorter videos, while videos watched by more engaged audiences can still produce clicks at longer lengths. That dataset is not specific to agency VSLs or appointment show rates. Use Arveno's booked-call and attendance data to adjust the durations.

## VSL script — “Make every enquiry count”

**Target:** 1:45–2:15, approximately 250 words at a natural 125–145 words per minute.  
**CTA:** Book a free 30-minute consultation.  
**Delivery:** Ahmad or another real Arveno team member, direct to camera. Warm, calm, specific. Record 16:9 for the website; create a separate 9:16 social cut rather than cropping important text into platform UI.

| Time | Picture / direction | Spoken script | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Direct-to-camera. No animated logo intro. | “If you run a contractor or local service business, you probably don’t need more marketing activity just for the sake of it. You need the right enquiries to turn into real conversations, estimates, and work your team can deliver.” | **Turn more of the right enquiries into real opportunities** |
| 0:15–0:36 | Cut to a simple customer path: search → website → enquiry → follow-up → estimate. | “That path can break in a few places. Customers may not understand what you do, the website may not make the next step clear, paid search may bring the wrong calls, or a good enquiry may sit too long without a response.” | Website · Search · Enquiry · Follow-up |
| 0:36–1:02 | Presenter with a simple diagram or real site/CRM screens, with private information hidden. | “Arveno Growth connects the pieces that fit your business: a clear website, search visibility, Google Ads when they make sense, lead capture, and practical follow-up. Where the data is available, we connect enquiries to booked estimates and the jobs they become, so the next decision is based on what is actually happening.” | **A connected path from search to booked opportunity** |

### Optional guarantee insert

Only include this in a longer VSL after the signed offer language and eligibility rules are checked against the exact package being advertised. For new agreements version 4+, setup is completed within 7 days of the agreed Setup Start Date or the setup fee is waived; unpaid setup installments are canceled. Older signed agreements retain their original 14-day term. The live-site “waived or refunded” wording is being reconciled against the signed agreement, so do not promise setup-fee refunds without checking the applicable contract. The separate monthly safeguard pauses future monthly service billing after the first three paid monthly fees if verified CRM-tracked gross profit from eligible projects has not covered those fees. Setup, ad spend, taxes, software and third-party costs are excluded; fees already paid are not refunded. Avoid compressing this into “risk-free,” “money back,” “guaranteed profit,” or “we work for free until you make money.” Put full terms beside the offer and in the agreement.

Suggested short insert, **only after legal/contract review**: “Our agreement includes setup timing and a conditional monthly-fee pause tied to verified CRM-tracked gross profit on eligible projects. We’ll show you the exact conditions and exclusions before you decide.”

## Pre-call script — “Your time is reserved”

**Target:** 0:45–1:00, approximately 115 words.  
**Placement:** Immediately after Calendly booking, above the meeting details.  
**Important:** The desired “booked but not confirmed” message needs a real confirmation step. Add a working **Confirm my attendance** action (or a monitored reply-YES process) and record the response before saying attendance is unconfirmed. A standard Calendly booking may already confirm the appointment; do not imply the booking can be cancelled for lack of confirmation unless that policy and automation actually exist.

### Version A — use only after the confirmation action is live

> Hi, I’m Ahmad from Arveno Growth. Your free 30-minute consultation is booked, but we haven’t confirmed that you’ll be joining yet. Please select **Confirm my attendance** below, then accept the calendar invite so the time and meeting link are easy to find. If your plans have changed, choose **Reschedule** instead. On the call, we’ll talk about your business, service area, how enquiries come in, and the kind of work you want more of. We’ll share the most useful next steps and whether one of our packages fits. No presentation or homework is needed. If you have your average job value or marketing budget handy, bring it; that’s optional. I look forward to speaking with you.

**On-page controls:** `Confirm my attendance` · `Add to calendar` · `Join the call` · `Reschedule`  
Show the booked date, local time zone, and direct meeting link above the fold. Do not make the person hunt through email.

### Version B — current-safe copy if Calendly already confirms the appointment

> Hi, I’m Ahmad from Arveno Growth. Your free 30-minute consultation is booked. Please accept the calendar invitation so the time and meeting link are easy to find. If your plans change, use the reschedule link in your invite. We’ll talk about your business, service area, how enquiries come in, and the kind of work you want more of. We’ll share the most useful next steps and whether one of our packages fits. No presentation or homework is needed. If you have your average job value or marketing budget handy, bring it; that’s optional. I look forward to speaking with you.

## Confirmation and reminder copy

**Immediately after booking — email subject:** Your Arveno Growth consultation is booked

> Hi {{first_name}}, your free 30-minute consultation is booked for **{{date}} at {{time}} {{timezone}}**.  
> **Join:** {{meeting_link}}  
> Please accept the calendar invite. If you need to change the time, reschedule here: {{reschedule_link}}.  
> We’ll discuss your services, service area, current enquiry path, and what you want to improve. You don’t need to prepare a presentation. Optional: bring your typical job value or current marketing budget if you know it.

**24 hours before — email or consented text:** “A quick reminder: your Arveno Growth consultation is tomorrow at {{time}} {{timezone}}. Join here: {{meeting_link}}. Need another time? {{reschedule_link}}.”

**1 hour before — consented text or email:** “We’ll meet in an hour at {{time}} {{timezone}}. Here’s your link: {{meeting_link}}. If plans changed, reschedule here: {{reschedule_link}}.”

Keep these as logistical reminders. Do not add repeated pitches, fake scarcity, penalties, or unsupported case-study claims.

## Funnel and measurement

1. **Instagram/TikTok short-form:** one business problem and one useful observation per clip. Keep the first seconds immediately understandable; use a real person, readable captions, native 9:16 framing, and a single CTA to the consultation page. Avoid manufacturing urgency or promising rankings, booked jobs, or revenue.
2. **Website VSL:** optional play, captions, no autoplay with sound, one CTA visible before and after the video. Do not require a full watch to book.
3. **Booking:** capture only the business facts needed to prepare. Do not add a long quiz after someone has already given those details.
4. **Confirmation:** place date/time/time zone, calendar action, meeting link, attendance confirmation if implemented, and reschedule action at the top. The video explains the conversation, not the entire package catalog.
5. **Reminder sequence:** immediate transactional confirmation, one reminder the day before, and one shortly before the call. Use SMS only when the person has agreed to receive it. For longer booking gaps, add one useful, relevant proof or FAQ message rather than a high-volume sales sequence.
6. **Judge quality by:** booked calls / booking-page visitors; confirmed attendance / booked calls; attended calls / booked calls; qualified attended calls / attended calls; and signed clients / qualified attended calls. Compare similar lead sources and time windows. Views and likes alone do not show whether the funnel is working.

## Research and what it supports

- **Booked-call pages:** VeloSync’s public page labels the appointment “not confirmed yet,” immediately tells the prospect how to accept the invite, offers short FAQs, then links case studies. This is a useful clarity pattern, not proof of an attendance lift. Softscale’s confirmation page states what the call covers, what the company needs from the prospect, and which business numbers to consider. Keep Arveno’s version much shorter.
- **Pre-call duration:** one public practitioner recommends 1-minute welcome videos and a broader multi-touch sequence; another recommends 1–3 minute FAQ clips and reports account results. These are practitioner claims, not independent controlled tests. Arveno should start with a single sub-minute briefing and only add FAQ clips after call recordings show repeated questions.
- **Overall video length:** Wistia’s 2026 report analyzes more than 13 million videos uploaded to its platform. It reports higher average engagement for shorter videos but also notes that longer videos can produce more CTA clicks among viewers. This is broad platform data, not a study of Arveno’s audience or VSLs. The 2-minute VSL is an editorial starting point; test it against a short page without a VSL when traffic permits.
- **TikTok:** TikTok’s current ad guidance recommends vertical 9:16, visible safe zones, sound, context captions, a clear hook/body/close, and continuous audience-specific testing. Its Top Ads library is filtered and only includes ads authorized for display; high-performer labels are not causal proof of a format.
- **Instagram Reels:** Meta’s Reels guidance favors native 9:16 video, quality audio, and keeping key text inside safe zones. Meta reports results from 15 split tests across several verticals and regions; these are ad-placement tests, not evidence that a certain VSL script will improve Arveno’s booked-call rate.
- **Social-video examples:** SOUP Agency’s Segway case-study page describes a presenter-led, real-world product walkthrough; Falter Media describes a TikTok/Instagram agency funnel. Their reported performance is self-published/platform case-study evidence and does not establish likely results for Arveno. The web research tools exposed descriptions and page copy, but did not provide reliable playback of the underlying Instagram/TikTok clips; no frame-by-frame or audio review is claimed here.
- **Appointment reminders:** a systematic review of randomized healthcare appointment studies found reminders can reduce non-attendance and that simple date/time/location details are effective. Healthcare visits are not sales consultations; use this only as directional support for clear logistics, not as a forecast of Arveno’s show rate.
