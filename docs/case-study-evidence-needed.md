# Evidence needed for quantified client stories

No auditable lead, spend, revenue, or return records for Seven Stones Landscape, Tridan Contracting, or IKAD Mechanical were found in the project materials reviewed. Keep the homepage stories qualitative until the following is collected and approved for publication.

For each company, record:

- Client-approved business name, service area, and project dates.
- Currency and the exact before/after date windows; use comparable periods and explain seasonality.
- Advertising-platform spend, agency fees, and other included marketing costs separately.
- Unique inbound leads, qualified leads, estimates issued, jobs won, cancellations, and completed jobs. Define each count and deduplicate calls/forms.
- Revenue actually collected or contract value, identify which was used, and document refunds/cancellations.
- Direct job costs and gross profit if describing profitability or a break-even guarantee.
- Attribution method and source exports (CRM, call tracking, ad platforms, invoices); disclose any gaps or estimates.
- The client’s written approval for the exact figures, quote, images, and publication.

Use explicit formulas in internal review: cost per qualified lead = included marketing cost / qualified leads; close rate = won jobs / qualified leads; attributed ROAS = attributed revenue / ad spend. Revenue ROAS is not profit ROI. A profit-based return should state whether agency fees and direct fulfillment costs are included. Never infer a result from a testimonial or fabricate a plausible-sounding number.
