How to use the ad budget calculator

Start with a budget you could keep running for at least three months; many local service businesses begin around $700 to $1,100 a month. Replace the example job value, close rate, and margin with your own numbers. The result is a planning scenario, not a forecast: real lead costs depend on your area, competition, season, and how fast new enquiries get a reply.

Ad budget calculator questions

Is this a forecast of my results?

No. It’s an editable scenario based on preset lead-cost assumptions and the numbers you enter. Actual costs and results vary.

Where do the lead costs come from?

Each trade uses a fixed planning assumption. Open “How this estimate is calculated” under the calculator to see the published comparison and its source.

Does the agency fee field mean you charge that amount?

No. It lets you include any marketing fee so the result shows your full marketing cost. Our pricing depends on your services and territory, and you see it in writing before you decide.
