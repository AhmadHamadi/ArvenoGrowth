Packages · FAQs

What does the setup fee cover?

If setup work applies, we explain the scope and payment schedule in the written agreement before work begins.

Is Google advertising spend included?

No. Google advertising spend is paid separately to Google. It is separate from the monthly service fee for Lead Engine and Growth Engine.

How does AI lead follow-up work?

Foundation Engine includes basic AI lead follow-up. Lead Engine and Growth Engine include advanced conversational AI follow-up, with lead qualification and appointment booking as listed.

Can I upgrade a package?

Talk with us about your goals and current scope. Any package change, updated fees, and start date are confirmed in writing before the change takes effect.

Does Foundation Engine require a commitment?

Yes. Foundation Engine has a 12-month commitment. The other package details above do not specify a commitment length; any term will be stated in the written agreement.
