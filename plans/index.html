Growth plans · FAQs

Which plan is the minimum?

Lead Generation is the focused starting offer. We’ll confirm that paid acquisition fits your goals, budget, margins, and ability to handle new enquiries.

Does Search Authority guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. We improve useful content, technical access, and clear site structure, but search and AI systems make their own decisions. No responsible provider can guarantee a specific ranking or citation.

Can I change plans?

Scope can change as business needs and evidence change. We discuss changes and fees before work begins.

Is ad spend included?

No. Platform spend is paid separately to the advertising platform. Service fees and recommended budgets are stated distinctly in the proposal.
