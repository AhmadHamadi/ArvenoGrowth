Seven Stones: the reported revenue change

The figures supplied for Seven Stones Landscape describe monthly revenue moving from 25k to 80k. That is a 55k monthly increase, or 220% growth; the later figure is 3.2 times the earlier figure. These calculations describe the supplied numbers. The currency, comparison dates, and underlying business records still need confirmation.

Revenue growth is not the same as advertising return or net profit. A return calculation also needs advertising spend, agency costs, the jobs attributable to those campaigns, and the cost of delivering the work. We have not published a lead count, close rate, or return-on-investment percentage without those records.

What the work illustrates

A landing page and an accurate business profile can make the next step clearer for a prospective customer. The page should explain which projects the business takes on, provide useful examples, and make a quote request straightforward on a phone. Consistent business details help customers understand whom they are contacting. Connecting the resulting enquiry with office records then supports a more useful discussion about booked work.

The landscaping marketing guide explains that customer journey in more detail. For the measurement side, see lead tracking and the guide to following an enquiry through a sold job. The homepage keeps the before-and-after client summaries easy to compare.

Questions about this work

Does this comparison show Seven Stones’ advertising results?

No. The slider compares website screenshots. It should be read as website work, while any advertising or revenue result needs separate measurement and source context.

Are the results typical for every business?

No. Every business has different demand, service area, budget, margins, staff capacity, and sales process. A client’s experience cannot predict another business’s outcome.

Why does this page label client feedback?

Attribution helps visitors distinguish a customer’s statement from independently audited analytics and makes the evidence easier to assess.
