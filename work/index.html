Questions about our work

No. Every business has different demand, service area, budget, margins, and crew capacity. One client’s results can’t predict another’s.

Why are the figures labelled client-reported?

The revenue numbers came from the clients themselves. We haven’t independently checked the dates or source records yet, so we label them clearly instead of presenting them as audited results.

Is the booked calendar a real client?

No. It’s sample data showing the kind of calendar view we set up. We only show real client numbers when the client has supplied and approved them.
